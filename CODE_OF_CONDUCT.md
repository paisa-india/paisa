# Code of Conduct

Paisa follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and patient; welcome people of every background, language and level of experience; criticise ideas, not people; and assume good faith.

Specific to Paisa:

- **No allegations or personal attacks** against officials, politicians, contractors or anyone else, in issues, pull requests, discussions or data.
- **No political campaigning.** Paisa is neutral; discussion stays on data, methods and code.
- **Respect privacy.** Do not post personal details of anyone, including contractors who are individuals.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer ([@amar-rokade](https://github.com/amar-rokade)) via GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove content and restrict or ban participants who break this code, as described in the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
