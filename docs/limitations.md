# Limitations and deployment blockers

The implementation is a usable national-finance vertical slice. The full supplied POC has not been completed.

- Pune municipal finance covers one year: PMC audited accounts FY 2024-25, as 18 revenue-account schedule totals (IN-1 to IN-9, EX-1 to EX-9). These are accrual figures, not the city budget and not cash collections. Capital expenditure, depreciation, the revaluation-reserve transfer, appropriations, the balance sheet, budgets and earlier years are not extracted.
- The PMC face Income & Expenditure statement (PDF pages 65, 91) is a scanned image. Parsed schedule totals are validated against a transcription of it in `connectors/pmc-accounts/reviewed-statement.ts`. The transcription was made during development and a maintainer should independently re-check it. A new fiscal year fails closed until its statement is reviewed and added.
- pmc.gov.in renders its document lists client-side, so the PMC connector checks one pinned, versioned file URL and cannot discover new files. Adding a year means adding a reviewed artifact entry.
- Verified local projects, tenders, contract awards and contractors are not connected. These pages intentionally expose coverage gaps.
- No actual contractor payments or end-to-end money trace is present. No names were synthesized to imply a real award relationship.
- National major-item expenditure is connected, but a ministry/department hierarchy and full scheme inventory are not.
- State figures come from RBI Statements 33–34. They are **ratios to GSDP**, not rupee amounts, so the UI shows derived "per ₹100" shares; rounding to 0.1% of GSDP can move each share by about ₹1. They cover the revenue account only (no capital spending). Five UTs without a legislature (Ladakh, Chandigarh, Andaman & Nicobar, Lakshadweep, Dadra-Daman) are not in RBI's study. Spending that RBI classifies as neither development nor non-development (up to 1.2% of GSDP) is shown as "Other spending". Rupee amounts need RBI's appendix tables (not imported). RBI's file server blocks scripted downloads, so a maintainer downloads the files each year. Official LGD codes are still null.
- Ask Paisa uses constrained matching, not a deployed language model. It does not support arbitrary multilingual questions or proportional tax queries.
- Hindi UI coverage is initial: key navigation, dashboard, calculator and guidance are translated; source labels, audit metadata and some detailed text remain in English. No human financial-glossary review has occurred.
- Responsive and keyboard behavior are implemented; a full WCAG 2.2 AA audit is outstanding. Do not claim conformance.
- Redis-backed scheduled code exists but a long-running unattended worker has not been deployed. Database migration and PostgreSQL adapter have not been exercised against a live database. S3 storage is not implemented.
- Failure handling writes logs, local health and issue drafts. No maintainer email/Slack delivery or GitHub issue submission is configured.
- Source reuse terms need review. Bulk export is disabled. AGPL applies only to project-authored code.
- No GitHub organization, remote repository, branch protections, required reviews, deployed production environment or production credentials have been created.
- The supplied brief ends in the middle of acceptance item 17 (“Have a connector…”). No additional text was assumed.
- Contracts cover **Assam only**, tenders 2019–2023. The data comes from a civil-society compilation (CivicDataLab, with the Assam Finance Department) of the state e-tender portal, not a direct government release, and is ODbL-licensed. Central (CPPP) and Maharashtra award data sit behind CAPTCHAs and are not connected.
- Award values are values at award, not payments. 237 awards with no usable value are excluded. Awards that fall outside 0.1–10× their tender estimate (often unit rates such as ₹ per trip) or have no estimate are listed but not counted in totals.
- The dataset's `statistics.value` field is inconsistent with award values (×100 to ×10,000) and is ignored. Its dates are YYYY-DD-MM and are converted.
- Contractors are grouped by exact published name (case and spacing ignored). People or companies sharing a name may be combined; spelling variants stay separate. Some contractors are individuals, shown exactly as named in official award records.
- Signals: 243 single-bid awards and supplier-concentration cases (≥60% of a department's checked awarded value, ≥5 awards in the comparison set). They are observations, not findings.
- Projects are MoSPI's central-sector projects of ₹150 crore and above, as reported by ministries (April 2026 report). State, city and smaller projects are not included. "Late" compares the original and revised completion dates in the same report, and "past due date" means the current due date is before the report month. Newer reports (May–August 2026) exist but sit behind MoSPI's script-blocked listing. Add their PDF links to `connectors/mospi-projects/index.ts` (`KNOWN_REPORTS`) or run from a network that can reach `ipm.mospi.gov.in`.
- Project signals: 242 cost increases of 25%+ (revised vs original approved cost) and 11 cases where spending ≥ 75% of current cost while reported work done is ≤ 50%. They are observations, not findings.
- City accounts come from cityfinance.in's standardised statements. They are accrual accounts, not budgets. Depreciation and provisions are bookkeeping entries, shown striped. cityfinance groups categories differently in some years (e.g. Pune 2023-24 puts the State's assigned revenue under tax revenue), so year-to-year comparisons of single categories need care. A year is published only if its items add up exactly to cityfinance's totals; others are counted as excluded. Totals of 0 mean "no accounts for that year". Reuse terms: permission requested; figures are shown with attribution and links to each city's cityfinance page.
- City dots use cityfinance's coordinates; a few cities have none and are not shown on the map.
