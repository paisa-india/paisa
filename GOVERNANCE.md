Maintainers review and merge pull requests, approve new data sources, and keep the project within its principles.

## Principles

Paisa is politically neutral. It does not rank parties or politicians, call spending good or bad, describe schemes as "freebies", or call any signal corruption. It shows official numbers, history, sources, automated signals and missing data. Decisions that would weaken these principles are not accepted.

## How decisions are made

- **Everyday changes:** a pull request with passing checks and one maintainer approval.
- **New data sources:** a maintainer confirms the source is official or openly licensed, that access requires no bypassing of controls, and that the licence allows Paisa's use.
- **Larger changes** (principles, licences, hosting, adding maintainers): proposed in a GitHub issue and open for discussion for at least seven days before a decision.
- While there is a single maintainer, that maintainer decides. Once there are three or more, decisions need agreement, and disagreements are settled by majority.

## Becoming a maintainer

Regular, careful contributors may be invited, typically after several merged pull requests including review of others' work. A second trusted maintainer will be added as soon as possible, so the project never depends on one person.

## Data automation

Daily data updates are proposed automatically as pull requests and must pass every check before merging. AI tools may draft code, fixes or plain-language summaries, but a maintainer approves every change, and AI never produces the published figures.
